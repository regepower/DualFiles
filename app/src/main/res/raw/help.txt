# Four pages
Swipe left and right (or tap the tabs): Tree S (folder tree of the source), Source (its files), Target, Tree T. Source and target have different colours and the letter S or T in the band. Copy, move, unpack and pack always go from the marked side to the other side.

# Open and mark
Tap a row to open it: a folder opens in the list, a file in its app. A file's icon shows the app that opens it; pictures and videos show a preview, music its cover (if the file has one). ".." goes one folder up, so does the back key; the list is back where you left the folder. Marking: tap the icon on the left (or hold the row), a check appears. While something is marked, tapping a row marks it too instead of opening it; once nothing is marked, a tap opens again. Mark a range: start on an icon on the left and drag over the icons up or down (the list scrolls at the edge). Drag slowly sideways (or with two fingers) to shift long names; a fast swipe changes the page. At the top of the list, pull further down and let go to read the folder again. Folders show the number of their sub-folders and files.

# Menu and actions
Hold a row to open a sheet at the bottom: name and size, then symbols for copy, move, rename, delete and a fitting quick action (archive: unpack, otherwise share); swipe down to close the sheet, the held file does not stay marked, then depending on the file open with, view (text/hex), print, unpack ZIP/7z (an APK as ZIP), pack as ZIP, copy to… / move to… (target, favourites, recent folders) and copy pictures as WebP to the target or convert them to WebP here (the original is deleted only after the conversion worked). With items marked by their check (tap the icon), the bar at the bottom shows count and size, symbols for all, invert and clear (hold one for its name) and the actions. Holding Copy or Move there also opens the destination list. Installing an APK needs the permission "Install unknown apps" once.

# Folder tree, favourites, sorting
The tree shows folders only, with the number of sub-folders and of files after the name, each with a small symbol (0 is left out, an empty folder shows nothing; hidden ones count only while they are shown). Tap a folder to choose it for this side (source or target) and to unfold or fold it – swipe over to see its files. The arrow only unfolds and folds. Top right: sort (list: name A–Z/Z–A with numbers in natural order (2 before 10), date new/old, size, type) and filter (pictures, videos, documents, archives, APKs, name contains …; the symbol is coloured while a filter is on) – both apply to the side that is shown. ⋮ top right: new folder, add the folder to the favourites or remove it (favourites are at the top of the tree with ★), target = source or source = target (the other side shows the same folder), swap sides, settings, help.

# Storage and network
The field on the left of the coloured band (e.g. "Internal ▾") picks the storage: internal, SD card, USB, cloud folders of other apps (☁, e.g. Google Drive, via "Add cloud / drive") and network servers (🌐). Below them the 5 most recent folders (🕘). Network servers: FTP, FTPS or WebDAV (e.g. Nextcloud with start folder remote.php/dav/files/NAME); "Search in Wi-Fi" finds servers that announce themselves. The connection is tested before saving, passwords are stored encrypted. Files from a server are loaded first to open them. The path is on the right of the band, every part can be tapped.

# Archives (ZIP, 7z, XAPK)
A ZIP or 7z archive (also the app bundles XAPK, APKS, APKM) opens with one tap as a small tree in the list (read-only): tap a folder in it to unfold or fold it. Other archives (RAR, TAR, …) open in their app like other files. ZIP and 7z from other apps (e.g. a mail attachment) can be opened with DualFiles too. Mark entries and copy (or hold → unpack): only these are unpacked, into a new folder named after the archive or directly into the target; the dialog shows the full destination path, the sheet below "To: …". Tap a file inside to open it. Password-protected archives are not supported.

# Text and hex viewer
View (text/hex) opens even very large files at once; as "DualFiles viewer" it is also offered by other apps under "Open with" (text and unknown file types). One finger scrolls both ways, two fingers zoom the text. Hold a word to mark it, the round handles extend the mark; then copy, share or all. Double-tap a word to mark and copy it at once. Top right switches between text and hex; ⋮ turns line numbers and wrapping (files up to 4 MB) on and off; in hex mode it picks 4, 8 (default) or 16 bytes per row and whether the right column shows only letters, digits and spaces (everything else as a space). Zoom (separately for text and hex) and settings are remembered. Known text formats (Kotlin, Java, C, JavaScript, Python, shell, JSON, XML/HTML, INI, YAML, SQL, CSS) get syntax colours, Markdown files are shown formatted (headings, lists, links; code and tables unwrapped, scroll sideways; two fingers zoom); both can be turned off under ⋮. Back first removes a mark.

# Copying, conflicts, progress
If a name already exists in the target, the app asks: overwrite (folders are merged), skip or keep both ("name (1)"), optionally for all. Copy, move, delete, pack and unpack show their progress and can be cancelled; a half-copied file is removed. Afterwards the app says what was done (e.g. "3 items copied to Download").

# Settings
⋮ top right → Settings, in groups: Display (switches for hidden files, previews including music covers, the ".." row, natural number order 2 before 10 – take effect at once), Apps (fixed app per file type, change or delete), Storage (add or remove cloud folders), Configuration (save and load: app choices, favourites, settings including viewer zoom and options), Problems (error report: last crash or hang, to copy or share).

# Access and privacy
The app needs "all files access"; the system setting opens on first start. No data is collected or sent. The app only goes online to servers you added yourself.
